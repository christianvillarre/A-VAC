Our Story

Family-Owned for Over 30 Years

A-VAC Septic proudly serves both homes and businesses with reliable septic tank pumping, cleaning, and maintenance. Based in Huffman, we're honored to support our surrounding communities with honest, dependable service.

For more than three decades, A-VAC Septic has been a trusted, family-owned and family-operated company. Founded by Karla and Fred Wolf, the business is now led by Jasmin and Famen Wolf, who continue the "mom and pop" values the company was built on — hard work, integrity, and genuine customer care.

Our commitment is simple: deliver quality work, provide honest service, and treat every customer like family. Whether you need routine septic pumping, commercial service, or an emergency response, we're here to help.

"You dump it, we pump it — and we've been doing it proudly for over 30 years."

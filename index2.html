A little septic know-how

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Planning maintenance or dealing with a problem? Start here.

How often should I schedule septic pumping?

A-VAC recommends cleaning every 3–5 years for many homes. Your tank size, household size and water use can change that schedule. Share your system details so we can discuss the right timing.

What’s the difference between pumping and cleaning?

Pumping removes the tank’s accumulated liquid and solids. Cleaning focuses on a thorough cleanout of settled waste and checking for visible problems. Tell us what you’re experiencing so we can help identify the service you need.

What signs mean I should call for service?

Slow drains, sewage odors, backups or soggy ground near the septic system deserve attention. These symptoms can have different causes; pumping alone may not resolve every problem. Call to discuss what you’re seeing.

Do you service commercial properties?

Yes. A-VAC provides septic cleaning and septic waste disposal for commercial properties, including businesses, RV parks, industrial facilities and construction sites. Explore commercial services.

How much does septic pumping cost?

Pricing depends on the tank, access and the work needed. Provide your location and any known tank details when requesting service so the team can discuss pricing with you.

Can I request urgent or weekend service?

Call (832) 318-2256 to check availability for urgent service. Office hours are Monday–Friday, 8 AM–6 PM; weekend calls and appointments are available by request.
